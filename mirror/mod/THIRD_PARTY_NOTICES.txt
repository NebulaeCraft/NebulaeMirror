mirror/generator/nebgen/ctm.py converts the texture metadata of the CTM mod 1.12
(https://github.com/Chisel-Team/ConnectedTexturesMod, GPL-2.0) and Chisel's "AR"/"SCTM" types into
Continuity / OptiFine CTM properties. It is an independent implementation of the tile layouts; no CTM or
Chisel code is included. Continuity (https://github.com/PepperCode1/Continuity, LGPL-3.0) is only a
runtime dependency of the client modpack and is not bundled.
